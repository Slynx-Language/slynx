# 4.1 Visit/Walk infrastructure (future/planned work)

**Status: planned — do not implement.**

This documents refactoring item 4.1 from `AcrossCodebase.md` as future work only.

## Problem

Every consumer that needs to recurse over HIR re-walks the tree by hand:

- `crates/hir/src/ownership/analysis.rs:355` — a manual match over every
  `HirExpressionKind`/`HirStatement` to compute ownership.
- `crates/hir/src/queries.rs:112-124` — `flatten_type` recurses by hand.
- `crates/codegen/src/styles.rs:166-191` — `flatten_struct_value` duplicates
  the struct flattening that `flatten_type` already performs.

New expression kinds silently break these passes until a panicking
`unreachable!()` or a missed case appears.

## Proposed design (not implemented)

- A `Visit`/`walk_*` trait (or generated `fold_*`/`walk_*` functions) over
  `HirExpression`/`HirStatement`.
- `flatten_type` as the single implementation reused by the ownership pass and
  codegen.

## Why this is deferred

This refactor introduces a broad new abstraction and would require migrating
every existing HIR consumer (ownership analysis, style lowering, codegen) to
the new walker in the same change. It is intentionally not part of the current
maintenance pass; the standalone deduplications that are safe on their own
(notably the unification of the AST-type → HIR-type walkers in `4.2` and the
shared enum-layout accessor) are handled directly instead.
